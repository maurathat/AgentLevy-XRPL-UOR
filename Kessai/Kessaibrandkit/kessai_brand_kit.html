01 / ESSENCE
What Kessai is
Every transaction is a question.
Every settlement is an answer.

Kessai is the settlement layer for the agentic economy. When two autonomous agents transact — one paying, one delivering — Kessai is what makes the moment of completion real. Payment escrowed. Work verified inside a TEE against a committed specification. Both released together, atomically, on-chain. No good-faith assumption. No after-the-fact dispute. A definite outcome, finalized.

The brand draws its identity from two parallel traditions of settlement. The Japanese hanko — the pressed seal that converts intent into legal finality. And the I Ching hexagram — sixty-four binary states, each a definite reading of an indeterminate situation. Both are technologies for collapsing ambiguity into a known position. Both are what Kessai does, in code.

The wordmark itself is a binary encoding — each letter a five-by-five grid of filled and empty cells, the same primitive as the hexagram glyphs. The brand is the system it represents.

03 / GLYPHS
Sixty-four canonical states
六 十 四 卦
The UOR alphabet maps every byte to one of 64 orbit-canonical states under a Klein four-group of symmetries.

Six binary lines per glyph. Sixty-four glyphs total — exactly matching the I Ching's hexagram canon, exactly matching a Braille cell's degrees of freedom, exactly matching the wordmark's per-letter encoding logic at a different resolution.

Three uses across the system. As identity marks, sealed inside the hanko at logo lockup. As state badges, indexing transaction status across product surfaces. And as texture, a quiet running pattern that says: this is a system that knows where it is.

08 / ARCHITECTURE
Master and products

Kessai is the company. AgentLevy and Witness are the first two products. Every product gets its own UOR glyph from the 64-state alphabet inside its hanko — same seal architecture, same palette, same typography, but a different canonical state. The brand family is held together by the system, not by repeated naming.

Master
Kessai is the company. The brand carries the Japanese cultural reference — the name (settlement), the kanji 決済, the hanko seal architecture, the I Ching glyph alphabet. It is the conceptual ground.
Products
Product names are English and functional — AgentLevy, Witness. They live under Kessai's visual system but do not carry Japanese names. Each product gets one UOR glyph as its identifier; that's where the family resemblance lives.
Attribution
Products carry "A Kessai protocol" or "A Kessai tool" attribution where context demands it — press releases, footers, legal copy. In conversational copy and product surfaces, the product name stands alone.
Glyph reservation
Master Kessai holds Glyph № 29 (The Settled). AgentLevy: № 56. Witness: № 21. Future products draw from the remaining 61 canonical states. The glyph is permanent and product-specific; once assigned, it does not change.
